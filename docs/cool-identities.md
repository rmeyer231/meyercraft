# Cool Mob Identities to Try

A cheat sheet of the most fun transformations in the Identity mod.

## S-Tier (Must Try)

| Mob | How to Get | Abilities |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| **Ender Dragon** | Kill the Ender Dragon in The End | Flight, huge size, intimidates all mobs |
| **Wither** | Kill the Wither boss (summon with 4 soul sand + 3 wither skulls) | Flight, withering aura, ranged skulls |
| **Blaze** | Kill Blazes in Nether Fortresses | Fire immunity, fly, shoot fireballs |
| **Warden** | Kill the Warden in Deep Dark (very hard) | Massive health, sonic boom attack, darkness aura |
| **Enderman** | Kill Endermen (common at night or in The End) | Teleport on demand, tall, pick up blocks |

## A-Tier (Great)

| Mob | Abilities |
|-----|-----------|
| **Ghast** | Fly + shoot explosive fireballs |
| **Phantom** | Fly at night, swooping attacks |
| **Iron Golem** | Huge health pool, massive melee damage |
| **Dolphin** | Super fast swimming, grace in water |
| **Bat** | Flight in tight spaces, tiny hitbox |
| **Creeper** | Charge and explode (you survive!), sneak up on mobs |

## B-Tier (Fun / Situational)

| Mob | Abilities |
|-----|-----------|
| **Spider** | Climb walls, night vision |
| **Slime** | Bouncy, split into smaller forms |
| **Chicken** | Slow fall (no fall damage), lay eggs |
| **Snow Golem** | Throw snowballs, leave snow trail |
| **Silverfish** | Tiny, fit through 1-block gaps |
| **Rabbit** | Jump boost |

## Pro Strategies

- **Exploring caves:** Use Bat (fly + tiny) or Spider (wall climb + night vision)
- **Ocean exploration:** Dolphin (fast swim + no drowning)
- **Boss fights:** Iron Golem (tank) or stay as player with good gear
- **Trolling your dad in LAN:** Creeper form, sneak up, explode, repeat
- **Building tall structures:** Enderman (teleport up) or any flying mob
- **Nether exploration:** Blaze (fire immune + fly) or Ghast (fly + ranged)

## Unlocking Mobs Faster (Cheats)

If you don't want to hunt every mob, enable cheats and use:

```
/identity grant @s minecraft:ender_dragon
/identity grant @s minecraft:wither
/identity grant @s minecraft:warden
/identity grant @s minecraft:blaze
/identity grant @s minecraft:ghast
```

Or grant everything at once by editing the Identity config file.
